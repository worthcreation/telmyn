One file per word for the word page, built by tools/lexicon.py.

Strong's dictionaries (1894; Open Scriptures JSON, CC BY-SA) for Strong's definitions, KJV renderings and root links.

n = verses the word appears in; refs = those verses (up to 60, spread across the Bible when there are more).
