# Purpose

## The name
Telmyn is a coined name drawn from two Greek words: telos (purpose, goal, completion) and meno (remain, abide, continue).
The spelling is new, so it has no established Greek translation. The meaning built into it:

Abide in Him. Become who He created you to be.

## What the app is for
Help people intentionally care for their whole lives, follow Christ's example, and grow toward maturity through faithful daily practice.

## Principles
| Principle | What it means for Telmyn |
|---|---|
| God's intention | Your life has purpose. Growth begins with understanding His design and calling. |
| Abiding | Stay rooted in Christ. Relationship with Him is the source of growth. |
| Alignment | Bring choices, habits, priorities and behavior into agreement with faith. |
| Whole-person care | Develop body, mind and spirit together, with attention to relationships and daily responsibilities. |
| Becoming | Cultivate gifts and potential while growing in Christlike character. |
| Sanctification | Let the whole of life increasingly reflect devotion to God. |
| Intentional action | Turn beliefs and good intentions into consistent, practical choices. |
| Perseverance | Continue through difficulty, return after setbacks, remain faithful over time. |
| Stewardship and responsibility | Take ownership of what God entrusts. Exercise discipline and cultivate it well. |
| Maturity and completeness | Pursue integrated growth: strength of character alongside physical and spiritual well-being. |
| Love and service | Express growth through humility, integrity, compassion and how others are treated. |

## Design rule
Tracking makes patterns and progress visible. It supports the journey. It never treats spiritual worth or holiness as a score.
